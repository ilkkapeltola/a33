Check before building

  • Dimensions are read off A21's 1:100 sales plan: 7.30 × 9.70 m inside, with the bathroom 2.34 m wide from 2.53 to 6.13 m back from the north wall. That gives about 71 m², against the 68.5 m² listed, so expect up to 10–15 cm less in places. The bed's clearances and the kitchen aisle have the least slack; a 160 bed gives 1 m each side.
  • The kitchen goes in the northern kitchen reservation (KT-varaus), where the sales plan shows one. The sink sits under the north-west window; confirm with EKE where the water and drain points are and whether the sill height allows a tap there.
  • A curtain stops light and sight, not sound. The TV and the bed share one room acoustically. A heavy, lined curtain on a ceiling track helps a bit; it's the trade-off for having no wall.
  • The bedroom's only window is its part of the south glazing. The sheers alone won't darken it; a blackout blind on that part would.
  • Cooking smells reach the eteinen and the wardrobes by the door. A good extractor matters more here than in version 3.
  • The parvi floor is at 2.22 m: 2.10 m below and 1.78 m above. Anything carried in from the front door goes down the passage under the parvi, so it has to stay under 2.10 m. The tall eteinen is where a sofa can be turned upright.
  • A21 is on the 4th floor, two floors lower than A33.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
